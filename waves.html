Wave Grid Practice

Read a wave on a grid. Up and down is amplitude. Side to side is wavelength. 1 square = 1 cm.

How to read a wave grid

  1. 1
    Find the rest line. It is the flat middle line, marked 0. The wave goes up from it and down from it.
  2. 2
    The vertical axis goes up and down. It shows the amplitude, how tall the wave is. Count the squares from the rest line up to the top of one crest. Don't count all the way to the bottom.
  3. 3
    The horizontal axis goes side to side. It shows the wavelength, how long one wave is. Count the squares from the top of one crest to the top of the next crest.
  4. 4
    Read the rule words. The same as means the number stays. One-half means a smaller number (8 → 4). Twice means a bigger number (2 → 4).
  5. 5
    One-half the wavelength = waves squeeze closer. More waves fit across the panel. Twice the wavelength = waves stretch out, fewer fit.
