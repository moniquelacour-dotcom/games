Grown-up: the timer counts clock time, but TimeBack only counts active time. When a subject reaches its goal, check its Active time on the family dashboard and type it into "TimeBack says". The bar then shows what is really left.

Press Go when you start a subject. Press Stop when you finish or take a break. Every minute counts. The little line on each bar is 10 minutes, which keeps your streak. Your TimeBack rings count something different, so they can look different, and that is okay.
