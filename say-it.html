What the marks tell your voice
  • ! — push. Louder, and a bit faster.
  • ? — lift your voice up at the end, like going up a step.
  • . — full stop. Drop your voice down and take a breath.
  • , — a tiny breath. Do not stop all the way.
  • … — wait. Count one in your head before the next word.
  • — — a sharp turn. Stop, then change direction.
  • CAPITALS — hit that word harder than the rest.

How to play this together
  • Take turns. One of you reads, the other guesses the feeling before looking at the chip.
  • Read it flat first, every single time. The flat one is what makes the good one sound good.
  • Push Try a different feeling and read the very same line again. Same words, new voice.
  • No timer and no score. This one is only about how it sounds.
  • Every character here is made up, and so is every line. Say who they remind you of.
